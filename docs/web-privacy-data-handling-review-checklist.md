# Web privacy and data-handling review checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist when a website collects, stores, uses, or shares personal or sensitive data. Legal requirements vary, so involve the organization's privacy or legal owner when a legal decision is needed.

## Understand the data

- [ ] Identify the systems, users, vendors, and personal or sensitive data in scope.
- [ ] Trace important data from collection through storage, vendors, backups, and deletion.
- [ ] Identify high-risk data and complete the organization's privacy assessment when required.

## Purpose and transparency

- [ ] Record why personal data is needed and remove unnecessary collection, copies, and retention.
- [ ] Confirm the site does not reuse or share data beyond the approved purpose.
- [ ] Keep the privacy notice understandable and explain important tracking or automated processing when required.

## Consent and user choices

- [ ] Ask for consent when required and make refusing or withdrawing it equally easy.
- [ ] Test that cookies, tags, servers, and vendors honor each privacy choice.
- [ ] Do not make a required service depend on unrelated optional tracking.

## Protect the data

- [ ] Limit access to personal data and protect it in storage, transit, backups, exports, and non-production.
- [ ] Keep secrets and unnecessary personal data out of logs and analytics.
- [ ] Do not copy production personal data into development or demonstrations without approval and safeguards.
- [ ] Review vendor responsibilities and any geographic storage or transfer restrictions.

## Retention, requests, and incidents

- [ ] Define how long each important data type is kept and where it is deleted.
- [ ] Test applicable privacy requests through the site and downstream vendors.
- [ ] Confirm privacy incidents have an owner, response process, and vendor contact path.
- [ ] Document legal holds or other approved retention exceptions.

## Finish the review

- [ ] Inspect actual data behavior and record unresolved legal questions separately from technical findings.
- [ ] Repeat the review when data, purpose, audience, vendor, or tracking behavior changes.
